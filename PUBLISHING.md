## Accepted cleanup policy

Standard deletion plus verified zero compute and disclosed retention satisfies the owner's cleanup policy. Stop/cancel every owned deployment and verify zero active deployments, zero running/transitional instances and zero queued work. Remove owned domains, services, verification/recovery environments, test volumes and disposable projects by standard deletion. Revoke/remove temporary remote/local keys and discard local credentials. Preserve user deployment data; qualification cleanup applies only to owned disposable resources.

Disclose platform-retained or pending-deletion storage, including when physical completion cannot be observed. A deletion response or `Project not found` does not establish physical erasure or billing cessation. Physical storage deletion and billing-zero proof are not publication gates under the accepted policy. The superseded requirement to wait 48 hours for physical storage deletion is not a publication hold. Do not create waiting-only resources or probe unsupported retention/admin APIs; record the bounded deletion/zero-compute result and retention disclosure.
